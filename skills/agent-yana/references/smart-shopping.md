# Smart Shopping

## What Success Looks Like
Fred either makes a confident purchase decision backed by real research, or walks away from something that wasn't worth it. Either outcome is a win. The goal is never to push a purchase — it's to make sure whatever he buys was the right call.

## Your Approach
Fred knows e-commerce — he builds it for a living. Skip the basics. Go straight to the signal: best option at what price, where, and why.

Default flow for any purchase:
- What is he actually trying to solve? (Don't assume the first product mentioned is the right category)
- What are the meaningful alternatives? (Not an exhaustive list — the 2-3 actually worth comparing)
- What's the price landscape? (Current price, historical range if available, when it's likely to drop)
- Are there smarter ways to buy? (Bundled with other needed items, cashback, marketplace vs. official store, subscription vs. one-time)
- Is this a now-decision or can it wait? (If Mercado Livre has a sale cycle or a seasonal pattern, say so)

For recurring items (household staples, spirits, etc.), check if subscription or bulk buying makes sense.

When the research is done and the answer is "buy now," present the cart ready — platform, link if available, quantity. He shouldn't have to click more than once to complete it.

**Price tracking:** If he's interested but not ready to buy, add the item to the Pulse price watch list in MEMORY.md with the current price and his target. YANA will monitor it.

## Memory Integration
BOND.md has his purchase patterns — price sensitivity by category, brands he prefers, platforms he trusts. MEMORY.md has the tracked items list. Check both before starting research so you're not retreading ground he's already covered, and so you know what he's already monitoring.

## After the Session
If something was purchased, remove it from the Pulse price watch list in MEMORY.md. If a product was researched but not purchased, add it to tracked items with current price and date. Note any preference patterns revealed in BOND.md (he always goes for X brand, he hates platform Y, etc.).
